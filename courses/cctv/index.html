Who is this course for?

  • New CCTV operators
  • Security professionals expanding into surveillance roles
  • Learners seeking an SIA CCTV-related licence-linked qualification

What will you learn?

  • Principles of working in the private security industry
  • Legal responsibilities, privacy and data protection
  • Control-room procedures and communication
  • Operational use of CCTV systems
  • Observation and surveillance techniques
  • Producing evidential documentation
  • Incident management and responsible information handling

Training and assessment

Assessment can include multiple-choice knowledge examinations and practical demonstration of CCTV equipment operation and evidential procedures.

Practical focus

Learners practise control-room activities so they can understand how surveillance decisions, evidence handling and operational communication work in a realistic environment.

Important: SIA licence-linked qualifications can include mandatory classroom attendance, practical training and assessment. Course delivery and licence requirements must follow the current qualification specification and SIA rules in force at the time of training.
