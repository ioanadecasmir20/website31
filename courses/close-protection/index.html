Close Protection Training

Specialist training for learners seeking to develop the knowledge, planning discipline and practical competence required for close protection work.

Duration18 days + 3 days FAW
Typical scheduleIntensive programme
First aid3-day Level 3 First Aid at Work
Book / EnquireCall 012 3826 147

Who is this course for?

  • Learners pursuing a close protection career
  • Security professionals moving into executive or personal protection
  • Operatives who need advanced protective-security skills

What will you learn?

  • Roles and responsibilities in close protection
  • Threat and risk assessment
  • Operational planning and route selection
  • Protective formations and team working
  • Venue and journey management
  • Surveillance awareness
  • Incident response and professional communication
  • Client care, discretion and professional conduct

Training and assessment

Close protection training includes demanding practical activity and knowledge assessment. Learners should confirm fitness, identification and prerequisite requirements before booking.

Course structure

The programme consists of 18 days of close protection training plus 3 days of Level 3 First Aid at Work, for a combined 21-day pathway.

Important: SIA licence-linked qualifications can include mandatory classroom attendance, practical training and assessment. Course delivery and licence requirements must follow the current qualification specification and SIA rules in force at the time of training.
