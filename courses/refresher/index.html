Who is this course for?

  • Existing licence holders approaching renewal
  • Door supervisors who need current refresher training
  • Operatives returning to licence-linked security work

What will you learn?

  • Updated security-industry responsibilities and current practice
  • Conflict management and de-escalation
  • Physical intervention and risk reduction where applicable
  • Emergency procedures and vulnerability awareness
  • Legal responsibilities and professional conduct
  • Incident reporting, communication and customer service

Training and assessment

Assessment normally includes knowledge testing and practical observation where the current refresher qualification requires it. Duration and content are confirmed against the specification in force for your licence route.

Why refresher training matters

Private security requirements change over time. Refresher training helps existing operatives maintain current knowledge, revisit safety-critical skills and meet the qualification requirements applicable to licence renewal.

Important: SIA licence-linked qualifications can include mandatory classroom attendance, practical training and assessment. Course delivery and licence requirements must follow the current qualification specification and SIA rules in force at the time of training.
