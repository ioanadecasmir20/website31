Who is this course for?

  • Security operatives who need first aid before licence-linked training
  • Workplace first aiders
  • Individuals who want practical emergency-response skills

What will you learn?

  • Scene safety and incident management
  • Primary survey and casualty assessment
  • CPR and use of an AED
  • Choking
  • Bleeding and shock
  • Seizures and common workplace emergencies
  • Calling emergency services and effective handover

Training and assessment

First aid is assessed through practical observation and questioning in line with the relevant qualification requirements. Learners must take part in practical activities.

Available pathways

Emergency First Aid at Work is typically delivered as a one-day course. First Aid at Work is typically delivered over three days and is used for the close protection pathway described on this website.

Important: SIA licence-linked qualifications can include mandatory classroom attendance, practical training and assessment. Course delivery and licence requirements must follow the current qualification specification and SIA rules in force at the time of training.
