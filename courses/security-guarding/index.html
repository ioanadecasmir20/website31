Level 2 Security Guarding Training

Core security officer training for learners preparing to protect premises, people and assets in commercial, industrial and public environments.

Who is this course for?

  • New security officers
  • People seeking guarding roles at offices, construction sites, retail or industrial premises
  • Existing staff who need structured licence-linked training

What will you learn?

  • Roles and responsibilities of security officers
  • Access and egress control
  • Patrolling and maintaining personal safety
  • Electronic and physical protection systems
  • Searching procedures and drug-related awareness
  • Emergency procedures and incident reporting
  • Conflict management and professional communication

Training and assessment

Assessment includes knowledge testing and any practical or observed assessment required by the qualification specification.

Course structure

The SecurWise pathway is four days of security guarding training plus one day of Emergency First Aid at Work. Typical classroom hours are 8:00am to 5:00pm.

Important: SIA licence-linked qualifications can include mandatory classroom attendance, practical training and assessment. Course delivery and licence requirements must follow the current qualification specification and SIA rules in force at the time of training.
