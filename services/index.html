Our training services

Professional security training for real operational roles

We provide structured security and first aid training for learners preparing to enter, renew or progress within the private security industry.

Classroom learning

Trainer-led instruction

Structured delivery covering legislation, responsibilities, communication, emergency procedures and the knowledge required for the relevant qualification.

Practical skills

Applied learning

Where the qualification requires it, learners practise job-relevant skills such as physical intervention, CCTV operation, first aid or scenario-based security procedures.

Assessment support

Preparation & guidance

Our training team explains assessment expectations, provides revision support and helps learners prepare for both knowledge and practical assessments.
